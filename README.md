## The New York City market exhibits high utilization pressure but suffers from significant spatiotemporal imbalance, 
# leading to high average wait times for riders.
# The data confirms clear, predictable demand peaks driven by commuter (weekdays) and lifestyle (weekends/late nights) patterns. 
# The primary strategic recommendation is to move from reactive surge pricing to proactive, time- and 
# location-targeted driver incentives and implement efficiency-boosting algorithms (like Forward Dispatching) to maximize vehicle output and reduce the system's high "Waiting Factor."
